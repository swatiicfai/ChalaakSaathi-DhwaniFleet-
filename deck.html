Overview

Brief about the Idea

Bridging the critical communication gap for 8+ million commercial vehicle drivers at Indian warehouse gates

The Target Setting & User

Users: Long-haul reefer truck drivers, fleet operators, and cold-storage dock supervisors.

Physical Environment: Chaotic highway tollgates, warehouse check-in barriers, and refrigerated distribution docks where diesel engines idle at 85+ dB, air horns blare, and drivers cannot pause maneuvering 16-wheelers to type English prompts or navigate smartphone forms.

Linguistic Reality: Drivers communicate in regional dialects (Hinglish, Punjabi, Marathi, Telugu) blended naturally with English logistics jargon ("slot", "reefer temp", "eway bill", "detention", "bay").

The AI Co-Pilot Solution

DhwaniFleet is a hands-free, real-time voice agent built on Gemini 2.0 Flash Multimodal Live that directly connects spoken driver vernacular to enterprise TMS (Transport Management Systems), IoT reefer telematics, and demurrage recovery ledgers.

Sub-650ms Latency: Instant natural turn-taking over bidirectional WebSockets.
Mid-Sentence Interruption: Handles guard shouts and driver corrections smoothly.
Real State Mutation: Executes atomic updates on warehouse slots and demurrage claims.

Gemini Integration

How DhwaniFleet Uses Gemini Enterprise & Agentic Design

1. True Multimodal Audio-to-Audio (No Cascading)

By avoiding traditional cascaded pipelines (Speech-to-Text → LLM Text Generation → Text-to-Speech), DhwaniFleet preserves raw speech acoustic features, intonation, and vernacular phonetic accents, reducing round-trip latency by over 70%.

2. Zero-Prompt Multi-Lingual & Code-Switch Auto-Detection

Gemini's deep contextual tokenization allows drivers to switch between Hindi, Punjabi, and English in a single sentence without pressing any language selection buttons.

3. Agentic Function Calling with Immediate Tool Feedback

Spoken intent directly triggers typed function calls (update_tms_dock_slot, log_reefer_temperature_excursion, file_detention_demurrage_claim). Tool execution responses feed back into Gemini's context within the same active turn.

4. Native Turn-Taking & Mid-Sentence Interruption (Barge-In)

The agent handles incoming user speech frames while generating output, halting generation instantly when interruption is detected and adapting to new instructions on the fly.
